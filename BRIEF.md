## Design Direction

### Vibe
- **Clean, modern, minimal** — think Linear, Vercel, Raycast landing pages
- Not a flashy agency portfolio. More like a well-crafted personal page by someone with good taste.
- Dark mode preferred (stands out, feels more "builder"), light mode optional
- Subtle animations (fade-in on scroll, hover states) but nothing distracting

### Typography
- Sans-serif, modern. Inter, Geist, or similar.
- Clear hierarchy: large bold numbers for metrics, clean body text

### Color Palette
- Primary: neutral/dark background
- Accent: one color for CTAs and highlights (something distinctive but not aggressive — a warm tone, amber/orange could work given Evaneos' brand uses warm tones)
- Metrics numbers in accent color for visual impact

### Layout
- Single page, scroll-based
- Responsive (mobile-first — recruiters often check on phone)
- No sidebar, no complex navigation. Just smooth scroll sections.

### Inspirations
- https://linear.app (clean, dark, purposeful)
- https://vercel.com (developer-focused, polished)
- https://raycast.com (modern, interactive but not excessive)
- Personal portfolio sites by PMs who build (not designers — avoid Dribbble-style portfolios)

## Key Principles

1. **Ship fast**: This is for an active job application. Done > perfect.
2. **Content is king**: The projects, metrics, and narrative matter more than fancy animations.
3. **Proof by doing**: The site itself IS the proof. Building a React app to apply for a PM AI role is the statement.
4. **No em dashes**: The user never uses em dashes. Use periods, commas, colons, or parentheses instead.
5. **English**: All content in English. The role and team work in English.
